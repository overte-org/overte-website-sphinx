Invitation to the Overte e.V. general assembly 2026
---------------------------------------------------

Saturday, November 28th at 19:00 UTC we will hold our annual general assembly for the Overte e.V. in Overte. While only active Overte e.V. members are allowed to vote, non-Overte-e.V.-members are also welcome to attend.

The planned agenda is as follows:

- Decide on assembly leader.
- Decide on assembly secretary. The secretary will fill out the protocol during the assembly.
- Accounting report for 2025 and 2026.
- Budgeting for 2027.
- Accounting auditor report (Steve Pruneau).
- Do we have someone that would like to become the accounting auditor? The accounting auditor cannot be a board member.
- Board report.
- Vote removing the requirement to pay previous years after a pause in membership. **[1]**
- Vote on accelerated board member elections. **[2]**
- Board member elections.
- Vote on LLM usage policy **[3]**
- Vote on exception from LLM usage policy for translation services **[4]**
- Protocol gets signed by the assembly secretary and assembly leader.


| **[1]**: Suggested change to § 3 Acquisition of Membership, Membership Fees (3.1) Non-payment of fees:
| From:
| Bei Beitragsrückstand ruhen die Rechte eines Mitgliedes bis der Rückstand beglichen wurde. (…)
| In the event that membership fees are not paid, the rights of a member shall be suspended until the fees have been paid. (…)

| To:
| Bei Beitragsrückstand ruhen die Rechte eines Mitgliedes bis der Rückstand für das laufende Jahr beglichen wurde. (…)
| In the event that membership fees are not paid, the rights of a member shall be suspended until the fees for the current year have been paid. (…)

| (Technically members owe the organization money from previous years. Meaning, if they pay for the current year but not for previous years, their membership is still paused. The proposed change aims to make it easier for members to come back after not paying their membership fee for multiple years.)


| **[2]**: Suggested addition to § 6 The Board (4) Election:
| (…) Bei Stimmengleichheit werden alle betroffenen Kandidaten gewählt, sofern ihre Anzahl höchstens der Zahl der noch verfügbaren Sitze im Vorstand entspricht und die Zahl der Stimmen mindestens 75 % der Zahl der Wahlberechtigten beträgt.
| (…) If there is a draw, and the amount of candidates that are drawing are less or equal to the amount of still available seats within the board, and the percentage of votes compared to eligible voters is >=75%, all of those candidates are elected.

| (This is to cut down on voting duration, since usually most candidates have close to 100% approval. Last board election, the lowest approval rating was 7 out of 9 votes; All of the candidates got elected.)


**[3]**: On 29th of March, the board voted to ban LLM-generated contributions ( https://github.com/overte-org/overte/commit/e84e20eacbb680237184f1ee610b7c4a23b349a1 ) coming to a consensus with the line being somewhere around full line autocompletion.
The entire discussion was very long, so the written rule is heavily simplified to avoid "bikeshedding". The reasons for the ban include copyright, ethical, and reputational concerns.
This vote is for checking if the assembly agrees in principle with the decision to disallow LLM-generated contributions to Overte. (This has nothing to do with people using generated code or assets in their own worlds.)


**[4]**: I am proposing to exempt translation services from the LLM usage policy for multiple reasons:

- All good translation services I am aware of make use of LLMs.
- Most of the concerns with general LLM-use are minimized for translation services:

    - The generated translations are generally very good;
    - Some of services believably claim to have trained their models without copyright infringement (there are large libraries of public translations available, for example from the EU; Services like DeepL claim to have been trained on their own proprietary dataset, which they have curated themselves over the years.)
    - The environmental impact is presumably *much* lower due to the clean datasets and more specialized functionality.

- Using translation services' suggestions greatly speeds up translating Overte documentation.
- Translation services' suggestions are good enough to act as a placeholder in case something hasn't been translated yet.
- Some of our users aren't fluent in English and use translation services (or general purpose LLMs) to report issues or generally communicate with us.
